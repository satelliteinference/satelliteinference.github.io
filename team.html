02 / HOW WE WORK

A small core, specialists when needed.

  1. NOW

    Founder and fractional CTO

    The founder runs the program, partners and customers; payload architecture and qualification plans are reviewed by an engineer with twenty-five years in space electronics.

  2. M1–4

    Technical cofounder, full-time

    Requirements, host interface and supplier quotations for SI-P1, then the build.

  3. M4

    Flight-software engineer

    SI Runtime on representative hardware and the Orbit-0 experiment.

  4. AS NEEDED

    Specialist contractors

    Avionics, thermal, radiation and machine learning, under IP-assignment agreements.

  5. BY QUOTE

    Test labs and host operators

    Chosen through competitive quotations, after checking each facility's qualifications and accreditation.

Timings are in months from the program start and may change.
