# Public program and evidence

Updated 4 October 2026 for v3.1-r1.

The current program combines SI Runtime, Orbit-0 on an existing orbital GPU, and a Thor-class hosted pathfinder. Development and test milestones remain targets. The base plan covers 13 months of engineering and qualification preparation; the SI-P1 flight needs separate funding.

The [current public baseline](/data/program-baseline.json) records the product, plan boundary and conditional roadmap. The selected future node model estimates approximately 96% annual sunlight in a dawn–dusk orbit, with seasonal eclipses remaining. The pathfinder follows its host's orbit.

The [publication manifest](/documents/manifest.json) lists public PDFs with their page counts and exact SHA-256 fingerprints; none is published at present. Company materials are shared directly. The whitepaper, detailed new engineering model, runtime mechanisms and private company or IP materials are not published on this website.

## Historical work

The [ground research record](research-results.md) preserves actual experiments with their limitations. The data-volume calculator illustrates arithmetic only, without predicting model quality, throughput, energy, or commercial value.

The former Rev C mission studies have been removed from the current website. Their inputs and equations are retained as superseded fixtures in `archive/rev-c/`; they must not be used as current mission assumptions. Existing tests continue to check their internal mathematical consistency.
