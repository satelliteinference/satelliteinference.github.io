CAPACITY RESERVATION

Reserve compute in orbit.

We plan a first 1 MW cluster in orbit in 2031 and target 1 GW by 2035. You can reserve a share of that planned capacity now. A reservation needs no deposit, creates no obligation to buy, and fixes your place in line by the date you sign.

The first years are small.

We plan 1 MW for 2031 and 9 MW for 2032. Capacity will be offered in the order of signature dates, so an early date is what a reservation is worth. You decide later, with flight results in hand.

  1. 01

    Reserve today

    Choose your capacity by year and ask for the memorandum. No payment is due.

  2. 02

    Follow the flights

    The first flight is targeted for late 2027. We report progress to you at least twice a year.

  3. 03

    Get the first offer

    When capacity is confirmed, we offer it to you before anyone with a later date. You have 60 days to decide.

  4. 04

    Confirm your years

    You confirm under a separate capacity agreement, with a prepayment expected at 10 to 20 per cent. Capacity is planned from 2031.

Choose your capacity.

Enter megawatts or a yearly budget for any year, and the other field follows. Each year’s capacity is planned to run for five years at the price of its year. There is no upper limit: ask for what you need, even above our plan.

What a reservation gives you

A place in line fixed by the date you sign, and the right to be offered your capacity first, with 60 days to decide. No payment is due, and you can withdraw at any time by email.

Who can reserve

Companies and public bodies in the United States and partner countries. We do not accept reservations from embargoed or sanctioned destinations, and every request is screened before we countersign.

What binds both sides

Priority, confidentiality, compliance with export and sanctions law, and governing law, from the day we countersign. Volumes, prices and dates do not bind either side.

Plans, not commitments

Planned volumes, dates and indicative prices are working assumptions. They depend on engineering, launch access, licences and new financing at every stage. A reservation is not an order: you confirm each year later, under a separate capacity agreement.
